What to mark

Listen and press K wherever a kill should land, the way you would cut a montage. Mark what feels right, not every beat. Press B for moments that deserve the biggest kill: an ace, a clutch, the drop.

Where a mark goes

Exactly where you pressed the key. Nothing snaps to a beat or a bar. The beat finder's grid is drawn only so you can compare. Slow the song to 0.75× or 0.5× to place marks carefully, or drag a mark in the views.

Keys

Space play/pause · ← → 1 s (Shift 5 s) · Z undo · click a mark, then ← → nudge 10 ms (Shift 50 ms), Delete removes it, Esc lets go.

Measure your tap delay

A click plays 100 times a minute. Press K exactly on each click for about twenty seconds, with the headphones or speakers you mark with. This only measures how late your presses land (your reaction plus the audio delay). Marks stay where you press unless you turn on “Shift marks by my tap delay”.
